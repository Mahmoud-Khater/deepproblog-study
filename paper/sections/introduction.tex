\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}

Breast cancer is among the most commonly diagnosed cancers worldwide and a major cause of cancer-related mortality in women~\cite{sung2021global}. Mammography and breast ultrasound are central to detection and diagnostic assessment. Machine learning and deep learning have achieved strong performance on medical image analysis tasks, including breast imaging classification and computer-aided diagnosis~\cite{litjens2017survey,balkenende2022application}.

Despite these advances, purely neural models can be difficult to interpret because the final prediction need not expose an explicit reasoning process understandable to medical professionals. In high-stakes clinical settings, explainability and trust are especially important~\cite{rudin2019stop,vandervelden2022xai}. One approach is to introduce an intermediate representation of clinically meaningful visual concepts and combine those concepts with explicit domain knowledge~\cite{koh2020concept}.

Neuro-symbolic AI provides a framework for combining neural perception with symbolic reasoning~\cite{garcez2002neural,garcez2023neurosymbolic}. DeepProbLog is particularly relevant because it integrates neural predicates with probabilistic logic programming, enabling reasoning under uncertainty and differentiable learning through the symbolic program~\cite{manhaeve2018deepproblog,manhaeve2021neural}.

The idea of this work is simple: keep a strong neural perception front-end, but route the final diagnosis through an explicit, inspectable reasoning layer. We study a neuro-symbolic pipeline on CBIS-DDSM~\cite{lee2017cbis} in which an ImageNet-pretrained ResNet18 trunk with four concept heads extracts lesion kind, shape, margin, and calcification type, and DeepProbLog combines those uncertain concepts with declarative rules inspired by BI-RADS terminology~\cite{acr2013birads}. DeepProbLog is added specifically for explainability: instead of a black-box $P(\mathrm{malignant})$ alone, every prediction decomposes into concept probabilities and which rule fired. A matched end-to-end ResNet18 baseline on the same splits provides the accuracy reference; we do not claim that DeepProbLog must beat that baseline on every metric. BUSI~\cite{aldhabyani2020busi} remains a candidate for later comparison.

The contributions of this study are: (i)~a DeepProbLog architecture that couples a shared ResNet18 concept backbone with lesion-kind-gated malignancy rules on CBIS-DDSM for explainable decisions; (ii)~representation of uncertain neural predictions as neural predicates inside a probabilistic logic program; (iii)~a patient-disjoint experimental protocol with class-balanced augmentation, ROI crops, concept warm-up, and a validation-selected high-sensitivity operating point; (iv)~quantitative comparison against a pure ResNet18 baseline, including why higher sensitivity comes with lower specificity at that operating point; and (v)~case-level reasoning traces that attribute errors to named concepts rather than opaque logits.
