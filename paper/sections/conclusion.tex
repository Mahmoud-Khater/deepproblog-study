\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This paper presented a neuro-symbolic breast cancer diagnosis pipeline that combines neural visual concept extraction with DeepProbLog-based probabilistic logical reasoning and medical knowledge inspired by BI-RADS. On patient-disjoint CBIS-DDSM evaluation, the model reaches test ROC-AUC $0.641$ and, at a validation-chosen high-sensitivity threshold, test sensitivity $0.848$ with specificity $0.326$. These metrics are modest; the contribution emphasized here is that DeepProbLog exposes how each decision is formed.

Unlike an end-to-end CNN that returns only $P(\mathrm{malignant})$, the proposed system reports concept probabilities, the strength of each rule body, and the strongest rule. Case studies show that false positives can be traced to misclassified lesion kind and false negatives to under-estimated shape or margin---failures of perception that the symbolic layer makes visible rather than hiding inside a single logit. Declarative rules also remain editable: gating calcification evidence by lesion kind corrected an earlier always-malignant collapse without relabeling data.

The neural component is responsible for perception; DeepProbLog provides uncertain symbolic inference; and the knowledge base encodes a working computational program, not official BI-RADS criteria. Future work includes a matched pure-neural baseline, stronger pretrained concept extractors (especially for margin), expert review of reasoning traces, and evaluation on additional datasets such as BUSI.
