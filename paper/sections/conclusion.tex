\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This paper presented a neuro-symbolic breast cancer diagnosis pipeline that combines neural visual concept extraction with DeepProbLog-based probabilistic logical reasoning and medical knowledge inspired by BI-RADS. Perception uses an ImageNet-pretrained ResNet18 trunk shared by four concept heads; a matched end-to-end ResNet18 classifier on the same patient-disjoint CBIS-DDSM splits is the accuracy reference.

With ResNet concepts, DeepProbLog reaches test ROC-AUC $0.700$ and, at threshold $0.5$, sensitivity $0.805$ with specificity $0.465$. The pure ResNet18 baseline reaches AUC $0.746$ and accuracy $0.683$, but only sensitivity $0.510$ at the same cut-off. Earlier from-scratch concept CNNs stalled near AUC $0.64$ even at $224$ resolution; transfer learning was the change that moved ranking and concept agreement (margin $0.51\rightarrow 0.70$).

Unlike an end-to-end CNN that returns only $P(\mathrm{malignant})$, the proposed system reports concept probabilities, rule-body strengths, and the strongest rule. Case studies show that improving perception fixes prior false positives caused by wrong lesion kind, while remaining errors are still attributable to under- or over-called BI-RADS descriptors rather than to opaque logits. Declarative rules remain editable without relabeling images.

Future work includes expert review of reasoning traces, stronger margin-focused supervision, and evaluation on additional datasets such as BUSI.
