\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This paper presented a neuro-symbolic breast cancer diagnosis pipeline that combines neural visual concept extraction with DeepProbLog-based probabilistic logical reasoning and medical knowledge inspired by BI-RADS. Perception uses an ImageNet-pretrained ResNet18 trunk shared by four concept heads, warmed up with class-weighted cross-entropy on CSV descriptors and then fine-tuned through DeepProbLog. A matched end-to-end ResNet18 classifier on the same patient-disjoint CBIS-DDSM splits is the accuracy reference.

The final DeepProbLog model reaches test ROC-AUC $0.752$. At a validation-chosen high-sensitivity threshold ($0.55$), sensitivity is $0.798$ and specificity $0.558$; a more balanced macro-F1 cut-off ($0.60$) yields sensitivity $0.716$ and specificity $0.633$. The pure ResNet18 baseline reaches AUC $0.746$ but does not attain sensitivity $0.80$ under the same protocol. Earlier from-scratch concept CNNs stalled near AUC $0.64$; transfer learning and concept warm-up were the changes that moved ranking.

Unlike an end-to-end CNN that returns only $P(\mathrm{malignant})$, the proposed system reports concept probabilities, rule-body strengths, and the strongest rule. Remaining errors are attributable to under- or over-called BI-RADS descriptors---especially lesion kind, shape, and margin---rather than to opaque logits. Declarative rules remain editable without relabeling images.

Future work includes stronger margin-focused supervision, expert review of reasoning traces, and evaluation on additional datasets such as BUSI.
