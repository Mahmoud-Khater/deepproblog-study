\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This paper presented a neuro-symbolic breast cancer diagnosis pipeline that combines neural visual concept extraction with DeepProbLog-based probabilistic logical reasoning and medical knowledge inspired by BI-RADS. Perception uses an ImageNet-pretrained ResNet18 trunk shared by four concept heads; a matched end-to-end ResNet18 classifier on the same patient-disjoint CBIS-DDSM splits provides the accuracy reference. With a from-scratch concept CNN, the inspectable pipeline already reached test ROC-AUC $0.641$ and, at a validation-chosen high-sensitivity threshold, test sensitivity $0.848$ with specificity $0.326$. Those metrics are modest; the contribution emphasized here is that DeepProbLog exposes how each decision is formed, and that traces correctly localize failures to perception (especially margin) rather than to the rules.

Unlike an end-to-end CNN that returns only $P(\mathrm{malignant})$, the proposed system reports concept probabilities, the strength of each rule body, and the strongest rule. Case studies show that false positives can be traced to misclassified lesion kind and false negatives to under-estimated shape or margin. Declarative rules also remain editable: gating calcification evidence by lesion kind corrected an earlier always-malignant collapse without relabeling data.

The neural component is responsible for perception; DeepProbLog provides uncertain symbolic inference; and the knowledge base encodes a working computational program, not official BI-RADS criteria. Future work includes reporting the ResNet18 transfer-learning and baseline numbers on the same protocol, expert review of reasoning traces, and evaluation on additional datasets such as BUSI.
